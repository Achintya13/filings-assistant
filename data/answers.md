## Q6

**Question:** In which quarter of 2025 was return on tangible equity lowest, and why?

**Answer:**

## Lowest Return on Tangible Equity in 2025

Based on the excerpts, the **third quarter of 2025** had the lowest return on tangible equity.

### Evidence

- **Q1 2025**: RoTE was **12.6%** ([2025-lbg-q1-ims.pdf p8])
- **First nine months of 2025**: RoTE was **11.9%**, or **14.6% excluding the Q3 motor finance charge** ([2025-lbg-q3-ims.pdf p9])
- **Full year 2025**: RoTE was **12.9%**, or **14.8% excluding the third quarter charge** ([2025-lbg-fy-results.pdf p16])

The fact that excluding the Q3 motor finance charge raises the nine-month RoTE from 11.9% to 14.6% — a swing of ~2.7 percentage points — clearly identifies Q3 as the weakest quarter.

### Reason

The drag was caused by a **one-off charge for motor finance commission arrangements** recorded in the third quarter of 2025 ([2025-lbg-q3-ims.pdf p9]). This was a significant exceptional item that materially depressed the reported RoTE for that quarter and pulled the full-year figure down to approximately 12.9%.

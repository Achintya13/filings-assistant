## Q4

**Question:** What drove the increase in the impairment charge in Q1 2025?

**Answer:**

Based on the Q1 2025 Interim Management Statement, the underlying impairment charge of **£309 million** in Q1 2025 (up from £57 million in Q1 2024) was driven by two main components [2025-lbg-q1-ims.pdf p8]:

1. **Base impairment charges** (excluding economic outlook updates): These rose to £274 million from £249 million in Q1 2024, reflecting underlying credit performance across Retail and Commercial Banking [2025-lbg-q1-ims.pdf p10].

2. **Updated multiple economic scenarios (MES)**: These resulted in a **£35 million net charge** in Q1 2025, compared to a **£192 million credit** in Q1 2024. This swing was a significant driver of the year-on-year increase. Notably, this included a **£100 million central adjustment** to address downside risks related to the potential impact of **US tariff policies announced at the start of April 2025**, which were becoming apparent around the balance sheet date [2025-lbg-q1-ims.pdf p8].

In contrast, Q1 2024 had benefited from a large MES credit of £192 million, making the year-on-year comparison particularly stark.

## Q7

**Question:** How did the CET1 ratio move between the end of 2025 and the end of Q1 2026?

**Answer:**

Based on the excerpts, the CET1 ratio moved as follows:

- **31 December 2025 (reported):** 14.0%
- **31 December 2025 (pro forma):** 13.2% *(reflecting the full impact of the 2025 share buyback announced in January 2026)*
- **31 March 2026:** 13.4%

On a **pro forma basis**, the CET1 ratio **increased by 0.2 percentage points** from 13.2% to 13.4%, driven by capital generation of 41 basis points (primarily strong banking build, partially offset by risk-weighted asset increases), partly offset by a foreseeable ordinary dividend accrual of 24 basis points [2026-lbg-q1-ims.pdf p9, p10].

On a **reported basis** (comparing the 14.0% statutory year-end figure to 13.4%), the ratio declined by 0.6 percentage points, though this largely reflects the buyback impact already captured in the pro forma figure [2026-lbg-q1-ims.pdf p4].
